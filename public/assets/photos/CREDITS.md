## Replacing one

Keep the filename, keep it 1600px or wider, and prefer frames that are already
dark or warm. Every photograph is graded (`img-graded`) and landed on the page
with a gradient rather than a hard edge, so a bright, high-key image fights the
treatment rather than sitting in it.
